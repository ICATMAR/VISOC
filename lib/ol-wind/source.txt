VISOC patches (search the file for "VISOC patch" - re-apply them on an update):
- WindLayerRender.prepareFrame: stops the animation and clears the canvas while
  the map is interacting/animating, and flags any view change (programmatic
  ones included) - the stale canvas used to flash up after a pan or zoom.
- WindLayerRender.renderFrame: after a view change, clears the canvas and
  re-seeds every particle in the new view - no segments or trail residue left
  from the previous view.
- WindCore.render / clearResidue: options `residueEvery` (frames, 0 = off) and
  `residueAlpha` - clears pixels the destination-in fade can never take to
  zero (8-bit rounding), which otherwise build up as a faint haze.
- WindCore.spawn (used by prepareParticlePaths and the respawn in
  moveParticles): particles are placed where the field has data (up to 20
  tries) - placed anywhere, those off a small field died on their first frame
  and respawned as one synchronized cohort. A particle's first life has a
  random age; every respawn after it lives a full maxAge.
- WindLayerRender.setData: clears the canvas before the new field's particles
  are seeded - the previous data's trails used to linger under the new ones.
- WindCore.fadeIn: option `fadeAlpha` (number or function) - the trails'
  per-frame persistence, separate from globalAlpha (the lines' opacity), so
  the trail length can follow the zoom. `residueAlpha` may be a function too.
