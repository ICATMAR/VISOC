VISOC patches (search the file for "VISOC patch" - re-apply them on an update):
- WindLayerRender.prepareFrame: stops the animation and clears the canvas while
  the map is interacting/animating, and flags any view change (programmatic
  ones included) - the stale canvas used to flash up after a pan or zoom.
- WindLayerRender.renderFrame: after a view change, clears the canvas and
  re-seeds every particle in the new view - no segments or trail residue left
  from the previous view.
- WindCore.render / clearResidue: options `residueEvery` (frames, 0 = off) and
  `residueAlpha` - clears pixels the destination-in fade can never take to
  zero (8-bit rounding), which otherwise build up as a faint haze.
