About

This app is developed by Gerard Llorach Tó ( ) (ICATMAR, ICM-CSIC) and is currently in beta. If you find any mistakes or have suggestions, please do not hesitate to contact me.

The app is open source and the code can be found at github.com/ICATMAR/VISOC.

The data presented in this app is for information purposes only. Although we strive to ensure its accuracy and reliability, we cannot guarantee that it is free of errors or interruptions, and it should not be used to make critical decisions without further verification.
